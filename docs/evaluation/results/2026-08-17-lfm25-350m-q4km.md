# LFM2.5-350M Q4_K_M cleanup evaluation

- Date: 2026-08-17
- Device: Google Pixel 7
- Runtime: Liquid LEAP 0.10.9
- Corpus: same 24 direct-text cases and three prompts as the 230M final matrix
- Raw result: [2026-08-17-lfm25-350m-q4km.jsonl](2026-08-17-lfm25-350m-q4km.jsonl)

## Outcome

**No-go.** The 350M model was slower and materially less conservative than 230M. It frequently
deleted or lowercased names/technical tokens, compressed questions into fragments, and changed
meaning. Most seriously, `do not send the final draft ... until I approve it` became `The final
draft will be sent to Alex once approved.`

350M's first download/load took 55,706 ms. Warm TTFT was generally 0.5–0.85 seconds and total
generation was usually 0.65–1.15 seconds.

## Representative failures from isolated rules

- `what time should we meet tomorrow` → `meet tomorrow`
- `send it to Sébastien and Mariko` → `send it to sébastien and mariko`
- `run ./gradlew ...` → `run gradlew ./gradlew ...`
- `do not send ... until I approve it` changed from prohibition to a future action.
- The Sarah → James self-correction remained unprocessed.

## Decision

Do not use 350M and do not spend another prompt round on it. Preserve 230M as the smaller latency
baseline, but neither tested model meets the cleanup safety/quality bar. Research the smallest
meaningfully stronger LEAP-compatible instruction model before proceeding to STT or integration.
